I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am advised by Professor Junxian He. Professor Junxian He has also advised me previously during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), from which I graduated in June 2024.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning, Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM), and LLM truthfulness and Interpretability.
